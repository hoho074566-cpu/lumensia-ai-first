# V0 Pure — Minimal Original Scene Core V2 Acceptance

## Baseline

This branch starts from exact Pure V0 `876e235918c99f4588c9ef4eb874ecee4541be97`.

It does **not** inherit the R2/R3/R3R/failed Pure Original Feel Writer prompt stack.

Human acceptance target is not “better” or “similar to the original”.

**PASS requires sustained play to produce the conviction: `이거 원작인데?`**

## What this experiment changes

Writer-facing guidance is deliberately small:

- resolve the exact current PC action first;
- move fast through anything that does not change the PC's immediate situation;
- when a person/world reaction starts, stay with that interaction;
- if a character has a concrete reason to act on the PC, let them actually act rather than merely watch;
- NPC↔NPC/background life may exist, but it must not turn the PC into a permanent camera;
- if nothing naturally changes, keep it short instead of inventing a side accident;
- do not chain unrelated incidents to prove that the world is active;
- connective prose stays lean; impact/tactical/relational changes may expand;
- one non-canon rhythm sample demonstrates pacing only;
- adaptive combat and persistent failure remain.

The failed abstract slogans from the previous attempt are intentionally absent.

## Retained non-narrative support

## Immediate CORE FAIL

- NPCs hold long independent scenes while the PC is only asked for an opinion at the end.
- The Writer creates chains like accident → NPC solves it → another accident → NPC solves it.
- Training-ground Canon characters notice interesting visible PC facts but only watch rather than act despite an obvious character reason.
- Every ordinary action is forced into a surprise or hook.
- Generic invented named students take recurring personal roles over plausible Canon cast.
- Several paragraphs of background description pass before the user's action reaches any consequence or useful landing.
- Prose remains uniformly polished, explanatory, and slow.
- Important NPCs become generic thematic-speech machines.
- Opening requires repeated meaningless continuation inputs.
- `08:55.` / `09:15.`-style state dumps appear as prose.
- explicit `사흘` becomes a different elapsed duration.
- Admin preview mutates canonical save/history/relationships.
- PC dialogue/private thought is authored without user supply.

## Strong PASS signals

A normal action quickly becomes a scene the PC can actually inhabit.

Examples of the **principle**, not required choreography:

- entering a place: a few useful details, then an NPC/person/problem that has a real reason to touch the PC's current situation;
- training: a character interested in technique acts on what they visibly notice rather than spending the turn sparring someone else;
- class: an instructor evaluates what the PC actually does instead of using the PC as a camera for five other students;
- quiet scene: genuinely quiet when nothing should happen, and brief rather than decorated with fake activity;
- combat: each exchange changes tactical possibilities and the opponent adapts.

Do not merge on isolated good samples. Judge the sustained run against the original references.
