# V0-R3C — PC In The Living World Acceptance

## Purpose

R3B produced a much more inhabited academy: Canon NPCs trained, disagreed, adapted, and reacted to each other without waiting for PC input. The remaining failure was that the PC could still become a spectator camera while the interesting scene happened beside them.

R3C keeps all R3A/R3B world-motion and Canon-cast gains while restoring one missing part of the original-feel loop:

> the PC is physically present inside the living scene, and plausible NPC/world initiative may naturally reach them.

This is not a protagonist-hook engine and not a requirement that every scene target the PC.

## Writer boundary

- NPCs may react to immediately observable PC facts: carried equipment, clothing, posture/technique, visible injury, current behavior, and public circumstances.
- They may not infer hidden abilities, exact ranks, private history, origin, name, motives, or other system-only facts without an established knowledge path.
- Quiet observation is still visible behavior. A character may notice being watched or may need another participant and initiate contact if that fits them.
- A quiet PC may also genuinely be left alone when the situation supports it.
- Over sustained ordinary play, the world should not remain interesting only among NPCs while the PC is indefinitely irrelevant.

## Human gate

Use ordinary actions without naming a desired NPC or requesting an event.

Suggested sequence:

1. `연무장으로 향한다`
2. `가만히 그들이 대련하는 걸 지켜본다`
3. `조금 더 지켜본다`
4. `점심을 먹으러 식당으로 간다`
5. `주변 사람들을 보면서 조용히 식사한다`
6. `오티 시간이 될 때까지 특별한 계획 없이 지낸다`

### PASS signals

- the training ground remains alive even before the PC is involved;
- at least some plausible scene contact reaches the PC when a character has a reason: curiosity about visible equipment/posture, need for a participant, irritation at being watched, a request, challenge, warning, invitation, practical problem, or another character-specific motive;
- the contact does not need to be friendly and does not need to occur in every scene;
- Canon Named NPCs retain their own relationships and conversations instead of instantly pivoting everything toward the PC;
- once contact begins, it can develop into a real scene and eventually a meaningful player decision;
- no system-only PC knowledge leaks into the initiating NPC;
- generic background life remains present;
- no exact reference choreography is copied.

### CORE FAIL

- multiple lively locations pass while the PC remains only a camera and all meaningful action belongs to NPCs;
- every scene forces someone to notice, recruit, challenge, or flirt with the PC regardless of plausibility;
- NPCs instantly know the PC's name, origin, abilities, exact skill level, or history from HARD FACTS;
- the Writer copies the reference's exact Lillia training-ground encounter or uses a fixed PC-hook pattern;
- world activity collapses back into NPCs waiting for PC commands;
- R3B Canon Named Cast priority regresses into invented recurring characters.

## Architecture boundary

No PC-hook score, attention meter, interaction quota, contact scheduler, event density controller, protagonist magnetism system, event queue, or additional model call. R3C is Writer permission + epistemic clarification only.
